Project Prompt: University Management System (UMS) ERP for COEP Technological University
Objective: Develop a comprehensive, fully functional University Management System (UMS) ERP software for COEP Technological University. This system should be a web-based application built on Firebase (or a similar backend-as-a-service platform), incorporating all the features outlined below. The application must be populated with realistic dummy data contextualized for COEP to demonstrate its full capabilities.
Core Technologies:
 * Frontend: A modern JavaScript framework (e.g., React, Vue, or Angular).
 * Backend & Database: Google Firebase (utilizing Firestore for the database, Firebase Authentication for users, and Firebase Storage for file uploads).
 * UI/UX: A clean, professional, and responsive user interface with a consistent design language and the COEP logo.
User Roles: The system must support distinct roles with specific permissions:
 * Super Admin: Has complete control over the entire system, including configuration and user management.
 * Student: Access to personal profile, academic information, courses, attendance, fees, LMS, and communication tools.
 * Parent: Can view their child's progress, attendance, and fee status.
 * Faculty/Instructor: Manages courses, attendance, assignments, grading, and research profiles.
 * Admissions Officer: Manages the entire admissions process.
 * Finance Officer: Manages fee collection, payroll, and financial accounting.
 * HR Manager: Manages recruitment, employee profiles, and leave.
 * Librarian: Manages the library module.
 * Alumni: Access to the alumni network and events.
Module 1: Admissions (Importance: Critical)
1.1 Student Registration
 * Description: Create a detailed and configurable student registration portal.
 * Functionality:
   * A public-facing registration page for applicants.
   * Admin panel to configure form fields, required documents, and program-specific questions.
   * Multi-step application form: Personal Details, Academic History (SSC, HSC, MHT-CET/JEE/GATE scores), Program/Branch Selection, Document Uploads (photo, signature, mark sheets).
   * Generate a unique Application ID upon submission.
   * Applicant dashboard to track application status (e.g., "Submitted," "Under Review," "Accepted").
 * COEP Dummy Data:
   * Programs: "B.Tech. in Computer Engineering," "B.Tech. in Mechanical Engineering," "M.Tech. in Structural Engineering," "Planning (B.Plan)."
   * Documents: SSC Marksheet, HSC Marksheet, MHT-CET Scorecard, Caste Certificate (if applicable).
1.2 Fee Collection
 * Description: Manage online fee collection during and after admission.
 * Functionality:
   * Define multiple fee components (e.g., Tuition, Development, Gymkhana).
   * Integrate with a payment gateway (e.g., Razorpay, Stripe) for online payments via UPI, cards, net banking.
   * Support for installment payments and application of concessions or scholarships.
   * Automated generation of fee receipts.
 * COEP Dummy Data:
   * Fee Structure for First Year B.Tech.: Tuition Fee: ₹85,000, Development Fee: ₹15,000, Other Fees: ₹5,000.
1.3 Scholarship Management (Importance: Important)
 * Description: A comprehensive module to manage scholarships.
 * Functionality:
   * List available government and private scholarships.
   * Allow students to apply for scholarships through the portal.
   * Admin dashboard to track applications, verify documents, and manage disbursement.
   * Integrate with the finance module to reflect scholarship amounts in student fee accounts.
Module 2: Student Information System (SIS)
2.1 Student Profile (Importance: Critical)
 * Description: Centralized and detailed student profiles.
 * Functionality:
   * Once a student is admitted, their application data should auto-populate a permanent student profile.
   * The profile should include personal, academic, and contact information.
   * A feature to auto-generate a professional CV/Resume using the profile data.
   * Track student progression year-wise, including academic performance and any disciplinary actions.
2.2 Course Management (Importance: Critical)
 * Description: Manage course registration, electives, and prerequisites.
 * Functionality:
   * Students can register for courses for the upcoming semester.
   * The system should enforce prerequisite rules.
   * Manage elective choices with seat availability caps.
   * Faculty can view enrolled student lists for their courses.
2.3 Other SIS Features
 * Attendance and Leave Tracking (Importance: Important): Faculty can mark daily/lecture-wise attendance. Students can apply for leave, which goes through an approval workflow.
 * Career Progression (Importance: Nice to Have): A placement portal where the T&P cell can manage company listings, students can build resumes, and apply for jobs/internships. Archive placement data.
 * Graduation Certification (Importance: Important): Configure eligibility rules for graduation. Generate rank lists, provisional certificates, and final degree certificates.
 * Mentoring/Counseling (Importance: Nice to Have): Map faculty mentors to a group of students for academic and career counseling.
 * Parent Interaction (Importance: Nice to Have): A dedicated parent portal to view their child's performance, attendance, and fee details, and to communicate with faculty.
 * Grievances and Feedback (Importance: Important): A formal system for students to submit grievances or feedback, with a defined workflow for resolution and tracking.
Module 3: Academic & Learning Management (LMS)
3.1 Curriculum Management (Importance: Important)
 * Description: Design and manage the university curriculum.
 * Functionality:
   * Define Programs, Courses, and Syllabi.
   * Implement Outcome-Based Education (OBE) by mapping Course Outcomes (COs) to Program Outcomes (POs).
   * Faculty can create and upload detailed lesson plans for their courses.
3.2 Academic Scheduling (Importance: Critical)
 * Description: A comprehensive scheduling tool for all academic activities.
 * Functionality:
   * Create and manage timetables, automatically checking for faculty and classroom clashes.
   * Schedule guest lectures, seminars, and workshops.
   * Manage faculty workload and assignments.
3.3 Assignment, Quizzes & LMS (Importance: Important)
 * Description: A full-fledged Learning Management System integrated with the ERP.
 * Functionality:
   * Content Management: Faculty can upload course materials (notes, videos, links).
   * Assignments & Quizzes: Faculty can create, schedule, and grade assignments and online quizzes. Include plagiarism checker integration.
   * Collaborative Learning: Discussion forums for each course.
   * Learner Analytics: Dashboards to track student engagement and performance on LMS activities.
Module 4: Exam Management System (Importance: Critical)
 * Description: An end-to-end system to manage all examination processes.
 * Functionality:
   * Scheduling: Create and publish the examination timetable.
   * Registration: Students register for exams; the system checks eligibility.
   * Question Paper Management: Create a secure question bank tagged by course and difficulty. Generate question papers.
   * Conducting Exams: Assign invigilators and manage exam day logistics.
   * Evaluation & Results: Enter marks, manage grading (relative/absolute), and process results. Allow for revaluation requests. Publish results on the student portal.
Module 5: Finance & HR
5.1 Finance (Importance: Critical)
 * Description: A complete finance and accounting module.
 * Functionality:
   * Accounts Configuration: Manage Chart of Accounts, ledgers, and cost centers.
   * Procurement: Manage vendors, purchase orders, and requisitions.
   * Fee Management: The central fee management hub from Module 1.
   * Payroll Management: Configure salary structures for faculty and staff, process monthly salaries, and manage tax deductions.
   * Budgeting: Plan, allocate, and monitor departmental budgets.
5.2 Human Resources Management System (HRMS)
 * Description: Manage all employee-related activities.
 * Functionality:
   * Recruitment (Importance: Critical): Post job openings, track applicants, and schedule interviews.
   * Employee Profile (Importance: Critical): Maintain detailed profiles for all staff and faculty.
   * Leave Management (Importance: Critical): Configure leave policies and manage the entire leave application and approval workflow.
   * Faculty Performance (Importance: Important): A system for annual performance appraisals, goal setting, and feedback collection.
   * Exit Management (Importance: Important): Manage the full and final settlement process for departing employees.
Module 6: Accreditation & Research
6.1 Accreditation (Importance: Important)
 * Description: A module to simplify the accreditation data collection process.
 * Functionality:
   * Generate reports required for NBA, NAAC, and NIRF by automatically collating data from all other modules (e.g., student-faculty ratio, publications, results).
   * Manage compliance with regulatory bodies like UGC and AICTE.
6.2 Research Management
 * Description: A dedicated module to track and manage all research activities.
 * Functionality:
   * Faculty Research Profile (Importance: Important): Faculty can manage their research profiles, linking publications from Scopus/Google Scholar.
   * Project & Funding Management (Importance: Critical): Track research project proposals, funding, and progress.
   * Publications/Patents (Importance: Important): Maintain a central repository of all research publications and patents from the university.
   * PhD Scholar Management (Importance: Important): Manage the entire lifecycle of PhD scholars, from admission and guide assignment to thesis submission and evaluation.
Module 7: Supporting Modules (Importance: Important)
 * Description: Essential supporting modules for campus operations.
 * Functionality:
   * Library Management: Manage the catalog, issue/returns, fines, and access to e-databases.
   * Hostel Management: Manage room allocation, hostel fees, and maintenance requests.
   * Transport Management: Manage bus routes, student passes, and vehicle tracking.
   * Inventory Management: Track all university assets, from lab equipment to furniture.
   * Alumni Management: A portal for alumni to connect, view events, and contribute to fundraising.
By developing all the features specified above, the resulting UMS ERP will be a comprehensive and robust platform tailored to the operational needs of COEP Technological University.
