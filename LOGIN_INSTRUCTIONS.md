# COEP University Management System - Login Instructions

## How to Access the System

### Step 1: Open the Application
- Open your web browser
- Navigate to the application URL
- You should see the COEP landing page with the university logo

### Step 2: Select Your Role
On the landing page, you'll see a dropdown menu to select your role:
- **Student**: For enrolled students
- **Faculty**: For professors and teaching staff
- **Administrator**: For university administration
- **Parent**: For parents/guardians of students
- **Finance Officer**: For finance department staff
- **HR Manager**: For human resources staff
- **Librarian**: For library staff
- **Alumni**: For graduated students

### Step 3: Enter Login ID
- Enter your assigned login ID in the text field
- For testing purposes, you can use any ID

### Step 4: Click Login
- Click the "Login with University Account" button
- You'll be redirected to Replit's authentication page
- Sign in with your Replit account
- After successful authentication, you'll be redirected back to your role-specific dashboard

## Test Users
Since this is integrated with Replit Auth, you'll use your Replit account to log in. The system will automatically assign the role you selected and create your user profile.

## After Login
Based on your selected role, you'll have access to different features:
- **Students**: View grades, attendance, fees, course materials
- **Faculty**: Manage attendance, upload materials, view student lists
- **Admin**: Full system access, user management, reports
- **Others**: Role-specific features

## Troubleshooting
If the page doesn't load:
1. Make sure the server is running on port 5000
2. Clear your browser cache
3. Try refreshing the page (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R)
4. Check browser console for any errors (F12 → Console tab)
